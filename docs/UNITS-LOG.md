changed, and how to undo it. Newest first. See `config/ai/context/capabilities.md` (Units).

Backups named below live on the Mac under `~/Projects/PowOS/backups/oneplus-open/` (not in git) or on the phone under
`/data/powos/backup/`. Phone hosts: `phone` = PowOS's root (`powos units`), `phone-android` = Android's side
(`sh /data/powos/powos-units.sh`). `powstation` = the Docker containers, checked from the Mac.
